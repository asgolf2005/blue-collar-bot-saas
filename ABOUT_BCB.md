# About Blue Collar Bot

## The Problem We're Solving

Every year, small trade businesses (plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs) lose thousands of dollars to:
- **Missed calls** - 30-40% of after-hours calls go unanswered
- **Expensive software** - ServiceTitan costs $1,500+/month with long contracts
- **Scheduling chaos** - Hours wasted on manual dispatching
- **Slow payments** - 45-60 days average to collect invoices

These businesses are forced to choose between overpriced enterprise software or piecing together spreadsheets and calendars. We built something better.

---

## What We Built

**Blue Collar Bot** is an AI-powered field service management platform that combines a modern CRM with an intelligent phone receptionist - at 70% less cost than competitors.

### The AI Receptionist (Our Secret Weapon)

Unlike any competitor, Blue Collar Bot includes an AI-powered phone receptionist that:
- **Answers calls 24/7** - Never miss another job
- **Understands requests** - Natural language processing
- **Books automatically** - Creates job + customer instantly
- **Sends confirmations** - SMS and email automatically
- **Saves $3,000+/month** - Replace receptionist salary

## Technology Stack

Built with modern, scalable technology:

- **Frontend:** Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, TailwindCSS
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime)
- **AI Integration:** n8n workflow automation
- **Payments:** Stripe
- **Maps:** Google Maps API
- **Hosting:** Vercel (edge network)

Our "Industrial Futurism" design language combines glassmorphic UI with industrial precision - modern but familiar to trade professionals.
